How the simulation works


While a tag is inside the reader's zone, the reader tries to read it several times. The number of tries is (reads per second × zone length ÷ speed), at least one. Each try succeeds with a probability that falls as interference rises (UHF is more affected by metal and liquids than HF). A parcel is captured if any try succeeds. Faster parcels and higher interference give fewer tries and lower success, and HF has a much shorter range than UHF. The numbers are model assumptions for a classroom simulation, not measurements from real hardware.


Assumed values. UHF: 25 reads/s, 10% success per try. HF: 10 reads/s, 55% success per try.
